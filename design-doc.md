## Problem Statement

The founder is building a service agency that offers three services to local businesses: (1) AI chatbot embedding on existing websites, (2) building new websites, and (3) building automation/AI workflows. The core pain is personal — the founder sees local businesses (dentists, salons, restaurants) losing customers because they can't respond 24/7, and existing agencies are expensive and slow. The immediate need is a professional public-facing website that showcases the agency's capabilities and captures leads, with a live chatbot demo embedded as the product showcase.

## Demand Evidence

- **Source:** Founder's own pain. No paying customers yet, no prior client work.
- **Market signal:** AI automation agencies are a validated business model with pricing from $500-$15k per project. Appointment booking chatbots and customer support automation are proven use cases with measurable ROI for local businesses.
- **Risk:** 80%+ of AI automation agencies fail in the first year. Main failure modes: pricing misalignment (clients want <$2k, real work costs more), selling generic tools, and poor client adoption.
- **Mitigation:** The founder is building the outreach tool first (the website IS the demo), not a full platform. This tests demand before heavy investment.

## Status Quo

Local business owners currently rely on agencies and freelancers for chatbot, website, and automation services. These are expensive (often $5k-$15k for custom chatbot integrations) and slow (weeks to months). Many local businesses simply don't have AI chatbots because the barrier to entry is too high. The founder's pitch: faster, more affordable, with a live demo that proves the product works before the business commits.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**Target user:** Local business owner — dentist, salon owner, restaurant operator, real estate agent. Someone running a physical business who needs online presence and customer support automation. They're too busy to answer every call, losing evening/weekend customers, and intimidated by technology.

**Narrowest wedge:** A general-purpose AI chatbot embedded on the customer's existing website. Not limited to appointment booking — handles questions, pricing, services, lead capture, and support. The chatbot is the hero service; website building and automation flows are upsell services offered once trust is established.

## Constraints

- Zero clients, zero revenue — this is pre-product.
- Founder is building solo.
- Must look professional enough for a local business owner to trust (a dentist won't hand their customer experience to someone whose own site looks amateur).
- No fixed pricing yet — "Book a Free Call" CTA for custom quoting until pricing is validated.
- Dashboard/internal tooling deferred until after 3-5 manual client deliveries.

## Premises

1. **Build the chatbot and landing page as one artifact** — the professional page IS the demo, the chatbot IS the product showcase. (Revised based on cross-model second opinion challenge.)
2. **Lead with AI chatbot as the hero service**, with website building and automation flows visible as supporting/upsell services.
3. **Dashboard waits** until after 3-5 manual clients reveal what actually needs automating.
4. **Professional, polished single-page site** with live chatbot demo, ready for cold outreach to local businesses.
5. **No fixed pricing on launch** — use "Book a Free Call" CTA, validate pricing through real conversations.
6. **The chatbot is general-purpose** — answers questions, books appointments, captures leads, handles support. Not limited to a single use case.

## Live Chatbot Requirement

The chatbot on the agency website is NOT a static mockup. It must be a real, interactive AI chatbot that visitors can talk to. This serves two purposes:

1. **Showcase:** Visitors experience the product by using it. "See It In Action" scrolls to or opens the chatbot. The chatbot acts as the agency's own AI assistant, answering questions about services, pricing, process, and helping visitors book a call.
2. **Proof of competence:** If your own chatbot works well, customers trust you to build theirs.

**Chatbot personality:** Friendly, professional agency assistant. Knows:
- What services AgencyAI offers (chatbot, website, automation)
- How the process works (book a call, we build it, go live)
- General pricing guidance ("pricing depends on your needs, book a free call to get a custom quote")
- Turnaround times ("most chatbots are live within a week")
- Can collect visitor name + email + business type as lead capture
- Graceful fallback: "Great question! Let me connect you with the team directly" → surfaces the contact form

**Implementation:** Custom-built using Claude Haiku API (`claude-haiku-4-5`). The chatbot is a React client component (`ChatWidget.tsx`) that calls a Next.js API route (`app/api/chat/route.ts`). The API route builds a system prompt with agency info and streams Claude's response back to the browser. The widget appears in two modes: inline in the hero section (the chat preview IS the real chatbot) and as a floating bubble in the bottom-right corner. No third-party dependencies, no watermarks, full design control. Cost: ~$0.0005 per conversation.
